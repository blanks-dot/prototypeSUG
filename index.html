Key Questions · Test with Mary
Watch For
Which step would she pay for separately? If she'd buy just the 2% prediction without the engagement, we can sell the prediction as a standalone product. If she only buys when engagement is bundled, the value sits in the conversion, not the data.
H1 · DATA ACCESS
Do you (or your partners) have the real-time data that makes this workflow possible?
H2 · PRICING THE PREDICTION
Would you pay for just the 2% prediction alone? What's it worth to you?
H3 · TRIGGERS & CONDITIONS
Do these triggers and conditions match your priorities? Any duplicates with your vendors?
H4 · COMFORT
Are you comfortable with us doing personalized outreach to your members?
Probes: "How would you want us to measure savings?" · "Where would this break operationally?" · "What would your CFO need to see?"

Key Questions · Test with Julien
Warm-Up · Before Showing the Prototype
Ask first, listen for 3–5 minutes: "Tell me how you feel about managing your health. What about your life right now makes it hard to take care of your condition — what feels challenging or overwhelming?" Note trust gaps, time/logistics constraints, and emotional state — these become the lens for everything that follows.
FRAME 2 · TRUST
Would you actually pick up? What would feel sketchy about this call?
FRAME 3 · INCENTIVE
Which would get you to say yes today? Rank them.
FRAME 4 · LOGISTICS
What does Maria need to do that we haven't shown?
FRAME 5 · FOLLOW-THROUGH
Would you actually show up? What would make you cancel?
Probes: "Where would this break for you?" · "What's missing?" · 24-hour recall test (strongest signal)
