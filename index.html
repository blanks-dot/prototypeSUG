Key Questions · Test with Mary
Watch For
How does she react to the $25k budget element vs. the trigger choices? This signals whether her primary lens is financial or operational — and what kind of vendor pitch lands first.

Key Questions · Test with Julien
Warm-Up · Before Showing the Prototype
Ask first, listen for 3–5 minutes: "Tell me how you feel about managing your health. What about your life right now makes it hard to take care of your condition — what feels challenging or overwhelming?" Note trust gaps, time/logistics constraints, and emotional state — these become the lens for everything that follows.
FRAME 2 · TRUST
Would you actually pick up? What would feel sketchy about this call?
FRAME 3 · INCENTIVE
Which would get you to say yes today? Rank them.
FRAME 4 · LOGISTICS
What does Maria need to do that we haven't shown?
FRAME 5 · FOLLOW-THROUGH
Would you actually show up? What would make you cancel?
Probes: "Where would this break for you?" · "What's missing?" · 24-hour recall test (strongest signal)
